In 2018 I sat down to build a personal website. I had plenty of ideas for it. They all stayed in my head, and the only thing that made it online was two words: "Hello World".

Writing was never the problem. I knew what I wanted to say. What stopped me was everything around the words. How would I design it? And once it was up, who would keep it running? I had no good answer to either, so the page stayed exactly as it was for eight years.

Building a site is far easier now. So this time I will design what I wanted to design, and write what I wanted to write.

More is on the way, and the gap after this post will not be another eight years.

Hello, world!
