The font files in this folder are modified versions of the Latin files Google Fonts serves for Archivo,
Newsreader and Permanent Marker (Archivo Version 2.001, Newsreader Version 1.003, Permanent Marker Version 1.001).
Each keeps only the characters the daylight saving post uses, with a margin, and Newsreader italic is fixed at weight 400.
They were cut with HarfBuzz's subsetter. Their licences are beside them: OFL-archivo.txt and
OFL-newsreader.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1), and LICENSE-permanent-marker.txt (Apache License 2.0).
